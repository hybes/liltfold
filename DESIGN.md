# Liltfold

A contact sheet for finding and gathering media already on your Mac. The media is
the main surface: a quiet header, one explicit filter bar, an airy virtualised
grid and a compact selection/export footer. No library import or cloud service.

Warm ivory (#F6F3EB), dark ink (#282822), amber (#94600C); charcoal (#232521)
with warm light text in dark appearance. System type for controls; a modest serif
collection heading. Six/eight-point spacing, restrained separators and rounded
media corners. The app icon and header use an original raster artwork of three tactile amber and ivory paper folds. Its source and generation prompt live in assets/.

Native AppKit collection cells keep fixed geometry as previews arrive. Opening a
preview keeps the collection alive and starts with its thumbnail; closing restores
the same scroll position. Short fades respect Reduce Motion. At narrow widths the
header condenses; essential filtering and export actions remain visible.

Finish condition: exercise real nested local files through browsing, preview,
selection, duplicate filtering and safe export; inspect light/dark appearances,
the compact window, errors and the export sheet. Record observations and measured
performance in VERIFICATION.md.
