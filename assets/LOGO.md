# Liltfold artwork

`Liltfold.png` is the production raster source, generated with the built-in
ImageGen tool on 7 October 2026. It is used throughout the native app and resampled
into the standard macOS `.icns` sizes by `tools/Icon.swift`. The generated alpha
channel is retained. No SVG or procedural mark is used.

The project artwork is offered under the repository’s MIT licence.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: finished raster macOS app icon and in-app brand mark for Liltfold, a beautiful local media browser.
Create one original, professionally art-directed app icon. The symbol is an elegant, sculptural fold made from three broad paper leaves gathered into a single folded corner, opening gently upward and to the right. Its strong, compact silhouette subtly suggests an L and the pleasure of unfolding a collection. Make the identity memorable and calm, with confident geometry and beautifully judged negative space.
Render it as a refined tactile object: warm honey-amber paper, a lighter golden fold and a small ivory inner face; precise folded edges, subtle paper grain, soft natural contact shadows and restrained dimensional highlights. Premium macOS icon craft, quietly ethereal, not shiny plastic.
Composition: straight-on, centred, square 1024x1024 asset. A softly rounded warm-ivory square tile fills about 90% of the canvas, with the dimensional amber folded-paper symbol filling about 68% of the tile. The area outside the tile must be genuinely transparent. Broad simple forms must remain recognisable at 24 and 32 pixels.
No letters, no wordmark, no text, no watermark, no mockup, no surrounding objects, no alternative icons or presentation board. Avoid thin line art, flat SVG-style strokes, generic folder symbols, sparkle decorations, heavy glow, glass, chrome, rainbow colour and complicated origami. Deliver a single polished production icon, not a sketch.
